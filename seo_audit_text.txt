The navigation menu links 'GULF FOOD ESSENTIALS' to /collections/chocolate (singular) while a separate collection /collections/chocolates (plural) also exists. This creates:

Confusing crawl paths — Googlebot wastes crawl budget on duplicate content.
RECOMMENDATION: 301 redirect /collections/chocolate to /collections/chocolates or rename one collection immediately.

No 'Recently Viewed' or 'Customers Also Bought' cross-sell — missing revenue recovery opportunity.
No pinned or highlighted review — even 1–2 reviews are not surfaced prominently enough for trust building.
